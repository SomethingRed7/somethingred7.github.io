使用说明

三种练法： ① 影子跟读——放慢语速跟读每句英文，模仿语气（每天出发前过一遍当天场景）；
② 角色扮演——和伴侣一人扮游客、一人扮导游/前台，把对话演一遍；
③ 场景预习——只读「核心句型」里的句子，当天到了现场现学现用。
小技巧：新西兰人说话偏快、元音发音扁平（fish→fush），听不懂就大方说 "Sorry, could you say that again?"，没人会介意。

A1Rotorua · 火山泥温泉8.29-8.30

📌 地热公园：硫磺味重、泥浆沸腾、90-100℃
游客
Wow, what is that smell? It's really strong!
哇，这是什么味道？好浓啊！
导游
That's the sulphur from the geothermal activity. It's completely natural and part of the Rotorua experience. You'll get used to it quickly!
那是地热活动产生的硫磺味，完全自然，是罗托鲁瓦体验的一部分。你很快就会习惯的！
游客
The mud pools are fascinating. How hot are they?
泥浆池太迷人了。它们有多热？
导游
The mud pools can reach around 90 to 100 degrees Celsius — that's boiling point. So please stay behind the barriers at all times.
泥浆池可达 90 到 100 摄氏度——已经是沸点了。所以请始终站在护栏后面。
游客
Can I take some mud home as a souvenir?
我能带点泥回去做纪念品吗？
导游
I'm afraid not. The mud here is protected, but you can buy processed Rotorua mud products in the gift shop — they're great for skincare!
恐怕不行。这里的泥是受保护的，但你可以在礼品店买到加工过的罗托鲁瓦泥制品——护肤效果很棒！
游客
Why does the mud bubble like that?
泥浆为什么会那样冒泡？
导游
The bubbling is caused by gases escaping from deep beneath the earth's crust. The heat comes from magma chambers far below us — it's a reminder that we're standing on an active volcanic zone.
冒泡是地壳深处逸出的气体造成的。热量来自我们脚下深处的岩浆房——提醒着我们正站在一个活跃的火山带上。
核心句型
  • How hot are the mud pools? 泥浆池有多热？
  • Is it safe to get close? 靠近安全吗？
  • Can I take a photo here? 可以拍照吗？
  • You'll get used to the smell quickly. 你会很快习惯这个味道。

A2Rotorua · 毛利村 Mitai8.29-8.30

📌 哈卡战舞、杭吉地炉餐、碰鼻礼
游客
Kia ora! I'm so excited to learn about Māori culture tonight.
你好！今晚能了解毛利文化，我太兴奋了。
导游
Kia ora! Haere mai — welcome! We're honoured to share our traditions with you. Tonight you'll experience our stories, our songs, and of course, our hāngī feast.
你好！欢迎光临！很荣幸与你们分享我们的传统。今晚你们将体验我们的故事、歌曲，当然还有杭吉盛宴。
游客
The haka gave me goosebumps. What does it mean?
哈卡战舞让我起鸡皮疙瘩。它代表什么？
导游
The haka is a traditional war dance, but it's also performed to welcome guests and celebrate important occasions. It shows strength, unity, and pride. The facial expressions — the pūkana, the wide eyes and protruding tongue — are meant to intimidate opponents but also to express passion and energy.
哈卡是传统战舞，也用来欢迎宾客和庆祝重要场合。它展现力量、团结与自豪。面部表情——pūkana，瞪大眼睛、吐舌头——是为了震慑对手，同时表达激情与能量。
游客
How is the hāngī food prepared?
杭吉食物是怎么做的？
导游
We dig a pit in the ground, place heated volcanic rocks inside, then put baskets of meat and vegetables on top. We cover everything with wet cloths and earth, and let the steam cook the food for about three to four hours. The result is incredibly tender with a unique smoky, earthy flavour.
我们在地上挖坑，放入加热的火山石，再把装满肉和蔬菜的篮子放上去，盖上湿布和泥土，用蒸汽焖烤三到四小时。成品非常软嫩，带有独特的烟熏泥土风味。
游客
The waka canoe on the river was beautiful — especially with the warriors chanting.
河上的独木舟太美了——尤其是勇士们吟唱的时候。
导游
Yes, the waka is central to our heritage. Our ancestors navigated the Pacific Ocean in canoes like these to reach Aotearoa, New Zealand, over 700 years ago.
是的，独木舟是我们文化遗产的核心。700 多年前，我们的祖先就是乘着这样的独木舟横渡太平洋，抵达了新西兰（Aotearoa）。
核心句型
  • What does the haka represent? 哈卡代表什么？
  • Can I take photos during the performance? 表演期间能拍照吗？
  • Thank you for sharing your culture with us. 感谢你们与我们分享文化。
  • Kia ora / Haere mai / Ka pai 你好 / 欢迎 / 很好

A3Matamata · 霍比特人村8.31

📌 指环王拍摄地：洞屋只是门面，绿龙酒馆免费饮品
游客
I can't believe I'm actually standing in the Shire! This is a dream come true.
我不敢相信我竟然站在夏尔！梦想成真了。
导游
Welcome to Hobbiton! This is a fully preserved movie set from both The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit trilogies. Take your time — there's magic around every corner.
欢迎来到霍比屯！这是《指环王》和《霍比特人》两部三部曲完整保留的电影拍摄地。慢慢逛——每个角落都有魔法。
游客
The hobbit holes are so small. Can we go inside?
霍比特洞屋好小。我们能进去吗？
导游
Most of the hobbit holes are just facades — the interiors were filmed in studios in Wellington. However, we do have a couple you can peek into, and you'll get to explore a full hobbit home interior at Bag End during the tour.
大部分洞屋只是门面——内部是在惠灵顿的影棚拍摄的。不过有几间可以探头看看，行程中在袋底洞还能参观完整的霍比特人家居内部。
游客
How did they choose this location?
他们是怎么选中这个地点的？
导游
Peter Jackson's team flew over this farm and immediately saw the rolling green hills and the party tree — they knew it was the Shire. The landowner, the Alexander family, agreed to let them build the set, and they've kept it ever since. Now over 600,000 visitors come every year.
彼得·杰克逊的团队飞过这座农场，一眼就看到了起伏的绿色山丘和宴会树——他们知道这就是夏尔。农场主亚历山大一家同意让他们搭建布景，一直保留至今。现在每年有超过 60 万游客。
游客
The Green Dragon Inn is amazing — can we try the special ale?
绿龙酒馆太棒了——能尝尝特酿啤酒吗？
导游
Absolutely! The tour includes a complimentary beverage from the Green Dragon. They brew a special Hobbiton ale, ginger beer, and traditional cider. Cheers — or as they say in the Shire, 'To the King!'
当然可以！行程包含绿龙酒馆的免费饮品。他们有特酿的霍比屯啤酒、姜汁啤酒和传统苹果酒。干杯——或者像夏尔人那样说："敬国王！"
核心句型
  • Is this where the party was filmed? 这里就是拍派对的地方吗？
  • The attention to detail is incredible. 细节处理得太棒了。
  • Can I get a drink at the Green Dragon? 能在绿龙酒馆喝一杯吗？
  • I'm a huge Lord of the Rings fan! 我是指环王的超级粉丝！

A4Waitomo · 黑水漂流9.1

📌 10-14℃ 洞穴水、萤火虫幼虫发光、装备全包
游客
I've never done anything like this before. Do I need experience?
我从没玩过这个。需要经验吗？
教练
No experience needed! We'll give you full training and all the gear — wetsuit, helmet, boots, and your inner tube. Just bring your sense of adventure and a towel.
不需要经验！我们会提供全套培训和装备——潜水服、头盔、靴子和漂流圈。你只需带上冒险精神和一条毛巾。
游客
How cold is the cave water?
洞穴里的水有多冷？
教练
The water is about 10 to 14 degrees Celsius year-round. The wetsuit will keep you warm — most people are surprised at how comfortable they feel once they're in.
水温全年大约 10 到 14 摄氏度。潜水服能保暖——大多数人下水后都惊讶于其实挺舒服。
游客
When will we see the glowworms?
我们什么时候能看到萤火虫？
教练
After we float through a few chambers, we'll turn off our headlamps in the glowworm grotto. You'll see thousands of tiny blue-green lights above you, like a living galaxy. The glowworms are actually the larvae of a special fly species unique to New Zealand.
漂过几个洞室后，我们会在萤火虫洞窟关掉头灯。你会看到头顶上方成千上万的蓝绿色小光点，像活着的银河。萤火虫其实是新西兰特有的一种蝇类幼虫。
游客
Is it completely dark in there?
里面是完全黑的吗？
教练
In some sections, yes — pitch black. But that's part of the magic. Your eyes adjust, and you become more aware of the sounds of the water and the glowworms above. It's a truly sensory experience.
有些路段是的——漆黑一片。但这也是奇妙的一部分。眼睛适应后，你会更敏锐地感知水声和头顶的萤火虫。这是一次真正的感官体验。
核心句型
  • Do I need to know how to swim? 需要会游泳吗？
  • How long does the tour last? 行程多长时间？
  • I'm a bit nervous — is it safe? 我有点紧张——安全吗？
  • Can I bring my camera? 能带相机吗？

A5Christchurch · 国际南极中心9.3-9.4

📌 风暴室 -8℃（体感 -18℃）、小蓝企鹅喂食、哈格隆德车
游客
Why is there an Antarctic Centre here in Christchurch?
为什么基督城有南极中心？
工作人员
Christchurch is one of only five gateway cities to Antarctica in the world. The United States, New Zealand, and Italian Antarctic programmes all operate from here. Scientists and support staff fly out from Christchurch International Airport to research stations on the ice.
基督城是世界上仅有的五个南极门户城市之一。美国、新西兰和意大利的南极项目都在这里运营。科学家和后勤人员从基督城国际机场飞往冰上的科考站。
游客
The storm chamber was intense! What temperature was that?
风暴室太刺激了！那是多少度？
工作人员
The storm chamber drops to about minus 8 degrees Celsius, but with the wind chill it feels like minus 18. And that's actually quite mild compared to an Antarctic winter, which can reach minus 60!
风暴室降到零下 8 摄氏度左右，加上风寒效应体感零下 18 度。而且这跟南极冬天比已经算温和了——那里能到零下 60 度！
游客
The little blue penguins are adorable. Are they native to New Zealand?
小蓝企鹅太可爱了。它们是新西兰本土的吗？
工作人员
Yes, the kororā, or little blue penguin, is the world's smallest penguin species and is native to New Zealand. The ones here at the centre are all rescued birds that can't survive in the wild due to injury or disability.
是的，kororā（小蓝企鹅）是世界上最小的企鹅物种，原产于新西兰。中心的企鹅都是因受伤或残疾无法在野外生存而被救助的。
游客
Can I book a Hagglund ride twice?
我能预订两次哈格隆德车吗？
工作人员
Absolutely! The Hagglund is an all-terrain amphibious vehicle used in Antarctica. It can climb over obstacles, cross crevasses, and even float on water. Let me check the schedule for the next ride.
当然！哈格隆德是南极使用的全地形水陆两用车。它能翻越障碍、跨越冰裂缝，甚至在水上漂浮。我看看下一趟的时间。
核心句型
  • When do they feed the penguins? 他们什么时候喂企鹅？
  • How do people survive in Antarctica? 人们在南极怎么生存？
  • Is Christchurch a gateway to Antarctica? 基督城是南极门户吗？
  • This is the closest I'll ever get to Antarctica! 这可能是我离南极最近的一次！

A6Dunedin · 奥塔哥半岛野生动物9.5-9.6

📌 黄眼企鹅仅存约 4000 只、皇家信天翁翼展 3 米、海狮保持 10 米距离
游客
I've heard the Otago Peninsula is one of the best places in the world for wildlife. What can we expect to see?
听说奥塔哥半岛是世界上最好的野生动物观赏地之一。我们能见到什么？
向导
You're in for a treat! The peninsula is home to the only mainland breeding colony of royal albatross in the world, as well as the rare yellow-eyed penguin, New Zealand fur seals, and sea lions. Each species has its own viewing area and best time of day.
你们有眼福了！半岛拥有世界上唯一的大陆皇家信天翁繁殖群，还有珍稀的黄眼企鹅、新西兰毛皮海豹和海狮。每个物种都有自己的观景区和最佳观赏时段。
游客
Why are yellow-eyed penguins so rare?
为什么黄眼企鹅这么稀少？
向导
They're one of the world's rarest penguin species, with only about 4,000 left. They're threatened by habitat loss, introduced predators, and changing ocean conditions. That's why we use specially designed tunnels and hides so visitors can observe them without disturbing their nesting sites.
它们是世界上最稀有的企鹅物种之一，仅剩约 4000 只。栖息地丧失、外来天敌和海洋环境变化威胁着它们。所以我们使用特别设计的通道和掩体，让游客在不打扰巢穴的情况下观察。
游客
The royal albatross is massive! How big is the wingspan?
皇家信天翁好大！翼展有多大？
向导
Up to 3 metres — that's over 9 feet! They're truly magnificent. They spend most of their lives at sea, only returning to land to breed. The colony here at Taiaroa Head has been protected since the 1930s.
最大 3 米——超过 9 英尺！它们非常壮观。一生大部分时间在海上度过，只有繁殖时才回到陆地。泰阿罗阿角的这个繁殖群从 20 世纪 30 年代起就受到保护。
游客
Is it safe to be around the sea lions?
靠近海狮安全吗？
向导
Sea lions look lazy but they can move fast if they feel threatened. The rule is to stay at least 10 metres away and never get between them and the sea. They're wild animals, and we're visitors in their home.
海狮看起来很懒，但感到威胁时动作很快。规则是保持至少 10 米距离，永远不要挡在它们和大海之间。它们是野生动物，我们才是它们家的访客。
核心句型
  • What time is the best chance to see penguins? 什么时候最有机会看到企鹅？
  • We need to stay quiet, right? 我们需要保持安静，对吧？
  • Its wingspan is incredible! 它的翼展太惊人了！
  • Is it safe to walk on the beach? 在海滩上走安全吗？

A7Te Anau · 米尔福德峡湾9.7

📌 全年约 200 天下雨——雨后瀑布最壮观；瀑布水是冰川融水可直接喝
游客
The scenery here is unreal. How was Milford Sound formed?
这里的景色美得不真实。米尔福德峡湾是怎么形成的？
船长
Over millions of years, glaciers carved these steep valleys, and when the ice melted, the sea flooded in. That's why the cliffs rise almost vertically from the water — some over 1,200 metres high. The water below is over 300 metres deep in places.
数百万年间，冰川雕凿出这些陡峭的峡谷，冰融化后海水涌入。所以悬崖几乎垂直地从水面升起——有些超过 1200 米高。下方水面在一些地方深逾 300 米。
游客
It's raining today — does that ruin the experience?
今天下雨——会影响体验吗？
船长
Not at all! Rain is Milford Sound's secret weapon. When it rains heavily, hundreds of temporary waterfalls cascade down the cliff faces. Some people say Milford is at its most magical in the rain. Just embrace it — that's what the rainforest loves!
完全不会！雨是米尔福德峡湾的秘密武器。大雨时，上百条临时瀑布从悬崖面倾泻而下。有人说雨中的米尔福德最有魔力。拥抱它吧——这正是雨林所爱的！
游客
Are those seals on that rock?
岩石上那些是海豹吗？
船长
Yes, those are New Zealand fur seals, also called kekeno. They love basking on the rocks. If we're lucky, we might also see bottlenose dolphins playing in the bow wave, and occasionally Fiordland crested penguins.
是的，那是新西兰毛皮海豹，也叫 kekeno。它们喜欢在岩石上晒太阳。运气好的话，还能看到宽吻海豚在船首浪里嬉戏，偶尔还有峡湾凤头企鹅。
游客
Can I drink the waterfall water?
瀑布的水能喝吗？
船长
We're actually going to get close enough to Stirling Falls that you can fill a cup — it's fresh glacial meltwater, some of the purest in the world. Many visitors say it's the best water they've ever tasted!
我们实际上会靠近斯特灵瀑布，足够你用杯子接水——那是新鲜的冰川融水，世界上最纯净的水之一。很多游客说这是他们喝过最好的水！
核心句型
  • How deep is the water here? 这里水有多深？
  • Will we see dolphins on the cruise? 游船上能看到海豚吗？
  • What's the difference between Milford and Doubtful Sound? 米尔福德和神奇峡湾有什么区别？
  • The drive here is as beautiful as the fiord itself. 来这里的路上和峡湾一样美。
